About

Hi, I’m Dillon.

Software engineer based in Irvine, CA, with a background in software QA engineering, automation, and game design.

My background in QA and design shapes how I approach engineering: thinking through edge cases, making failures easier to diagnose, and building systems that other people can understand and maintain. Game design also taught me to consider how people interact with those systems and where friction can be reduced.

Working across Diablo IV and World of Warcraft taught me how to collaborate with developers, designers, and production teams. I’ve always enjoyed working alongside teammates to solve problems and create something we’re all proud of. I have a strong passion for quality and creating polished products, so this line of work has always called to me.

I continue to build full-stack and backend-focused projects because I enjoy solving problems and learning through hands-on work. Whether I’m creating an addon for a game I’m playing or experimenting with a new tool, I’m just curious about how things work and how I can make them better. I bring that same curiosity to a team, along with a willingness to share what I learn and support the people around me.

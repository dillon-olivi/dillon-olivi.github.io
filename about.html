How ar'ya!

I'm Dillon — DJ to most people. I'm based in Irvine, CA, and I split my time between two things I care about equally: building solid, well-tested software, and designing the kind of game systems that make people lose track of time.

World of Warcraft has been with me since I was a kid, and it's honestly the single biggest influence on how I think about design — I play both Classic and Retail, chase high-end content, and still slow down for a good quest text or a gathering profession when the mood strikes. I'm an active Old School RuneScape player too, so if you catch me clicking a rock repeatedly for no good reason, that's why.

I hold a B.S. in Game Design from Full Sail University, and I spent time at Blizzard Entertainment as a Senior QA Analyst and Design Mentee on Diablo IV and World of Warcraft — QA automation, LUA scripting, and quest, item, and spell design. These days I'm building out personal projects across both software engineering and game design, which is exactly what this site is for.

Keep yer feet on the ground.
